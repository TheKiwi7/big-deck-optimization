# Frame rate per combat phase (fpsstat), starting deck (10 cards), the baseline: idle, plays, end of turn, idle with the mod off, deck view,
# rewards screen. Release-like timings (Debug re-checks off).
timeout 1200
newrun NECROBINDER
waitfor run 120
wait 2
cmd bdperf checks off
enemyhp 999999
cmd fight CULTISTS_NORMAL
waitfor idle 600
cmd bigdeck tank
wait 2
fpsstat
wait 10
fpsstat combat idle
play 4
waitfor idle 120
fpsstat plays
endturn
waitfor idle 300
fpsstat end of turn
wait 2
fpsstat
cmd bdopt off
wait 10
fpsstat combat idle, mod off
cmd bdopt on
wait 2
fpsstat
deckview 6
fpsstat deck view
cmd kill all
waitfor nocombat 300
wait 3
fpsstat
wait 10
fpsstat rewards screen
quit
