# ("checksum x" / "checksum x vanilla" must print the same value). ~4,700 cards + the reference relics, plays over turns (piles moving).
# Moved piles off (snapmoved off): the reference of snapcheck-5k-b (compare-with), its CHECKSUM and STATE lines line for line.
# Part b of the 7-turn check (formerly snapcheck-5k, one session of ~8 min: each vanilla checksum takes ~7 s at this size, the
# plays ~1 s a turn): the same seed and plays as part a (snapcheck-5k-a) up to turn 3's end, with its optimized checksums but not
# its vanilla ones (part a compares those), then turns 4-7 with every step's vanilla checksum. A vanilla checksum turns the whole
# mod off and back on: turn 4's first comparison here follows three turns of optimized checksums alone, a state no step of the
# one-session check had.
# 7 turns in all, the same on both sides of each pair (compare.py wants the same lines). Not more: `immortal` only clamps
# Creature.LoseHpInternal, while Doom (most likely: No Escape adds half the Doom already there, v0.113 also checks it after
# the player's turn) kills through CreatureCmd.Kill once it reaches the enemy's HP: on v0.113.1 the Calcified Cultist is
# gone before turn 7's enemy turn, the Damp Cultist dies at turn 8's end, the combat is won and the next 'waitfor idle'
# waits out its 300 s.
